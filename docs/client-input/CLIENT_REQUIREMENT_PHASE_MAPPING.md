## Restricted decisions

- Personal WhatsApp QR/unofficial WhatsApp Web automation is blocked; use the official WhatsApp
  Business Platform or Cloud API after approval.
- SIM/mobile call recording via accessibility abuse, hidden microphone capture or restricted
  permission bypasses is prohibited; prefer provider-side business recording.
- AI cannot silently overwrite authoritative CRM fields or publish content without human approval.
- Sales pipeline, operational module status and department readiness remain separate models.

## 2026-08-08 Phase 4 manual-recording amendment

The client change is mapped to Phase 4 only: users may upload a lawfully obtained recording for an
authorized canonical Lead/Contact when active recording consent exists. The CRM stores provenance as
`MANUAL_UPLOAD`; provider recordings remain `PROVIDER`. Both use one access, retention, activity and
audit model. Audio binary stays in private object storage, not PostgreSQL.

The amendment does not authorize SIM/mobile capture, call-log/SMS/contact/accessibility permissions,
AI transcript/summary, Previous Voice Quick Note, AI auto-calling, a duplicate customer/manager
model, or generic contact-profile/custom-field work. Those remain restricted or Phase 13 work.
